Traditional Dhokla — 4 servings

Ingredients:

1 cup rice
½ cup chana dal
½ cup yogurt
1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste
Salt
½ tsp turmeric
1 tsp sugar
1 tsp Eno/baking soda (added just before steaming)

Method:

Soak rice and chana dal separately for 5–6 hours.
Grind them into a slightly coarse batter.
Mix with yogurt and let it ferment for 8–12 hours.
Add ginger-chilli, salt, turmeric and sugar.
Just before steaming, mix in Eno/baking soda gently.
Pour into a greased tray and steam for 15–20 minutes.
Let it cool slightly and cut into pieces.
Add the mustard-seed tempering and serve with green chutney.
