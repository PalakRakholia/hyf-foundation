Veg Khow Suey — 3–4 servings

Ingredients:

1 tbsp oil
1 onion, chopped
3 garlic cloves + 1-inch ginger
1–2 green chillies
1 tsp turmeric
1 tsp chilli powder
1 tbsp curry powder
400 ml coconut milk
300 ml vegetable stock/water
2–3 cups mixed vegetables
1 tbsp soy sauce
Salt + lime juice
Noodles

Method:

Sauté onion, garlic, ginger and chilli.
Add spices and cook for 30 seconds.
Add vegetables, coconut milk and stock.
Simmer for 10–15 minutes.
Add soy sauce, salt and lime juice.
Serve over noodles with crispy onions, coriander, chilli and lime.
